01 / RESEARCH

My research lives at the intersection of embodied intelligence and real-world reliability.

I’m interested in robots that do more than execute motion: systems that perceive open-world environments, remember what they have seen, reason over changes, and act safely under uncertainty.

01

PRIMARY FOCUS

Semantic Memory & Spatial Intelligence

Persistent object-centric world models that fuse live observations with long-term memory—tracking identity, location, state changes, confidence, and history across time.

  • Open-vocabulary perception
  • Scene graphs
  • Vision-language reasoning
  • Episodic memory
02

REAL-WORLD AUTONOMY

Humanoid Navigation & Localization

LiDAR–inertial state estimation, dynamic costmaps, reactive planning, and safe navigation for humanoids operating in cluttered indoor spaces.

  • FAST-LIO2
  • KISS-ICP
  • MPPI + A*
  • Sensor fusion
03

ROBOT LEARNING

Learning for Manipulation

Simulation-scale policy learning for contact-rich manipulation, with robust environments, reset distributions, and randomization for sim-to-real transfer.

  • Reinforcement learning
  • Isaac Sim
  • Sim-to-real
  • Foundation models
