Nanditha Naik

Robotics Research Engineer · San Francisco, CA

I am a Founding Member of Technical Staff at a stealth humanoid robotics startup. I build autonomy and learning systems for general-purpose humanoid robots, with a focus on semantic memory, navigation, localization, perception, and robot learning.

Previously, I was a Graduate Research Assistant at the University of Minnesota, where I worked on 3D perception, generative modeling, and operational 5G/NextG systems. I received my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Minnesota.

Research Interests

My interests lie at the intersection of robotics, computer vision, and machine learning. I am particularly interested in:

  • Persistent semantic and episodic memory for embodied agents
  • Open-world perception and vision-language reasoning
  • Humanoid navigation, localization, and sensor fusion
  • Robot learning, diffusion policies, and sim-to-real transfer

Selected Projects

Persistent Semantic Memory for Humanoid Robots

Developed a live and long-term world model that tracks object identity, location, confidence, and history. The system combines open-vocabulary detection, segmentation, visual embeddings, VLM reasoning, and navigation to semantic goals.

Scene graphs · SAM · SigLIP · VLMs · RGB-D · ROS 2

Visuomotor Diffusion Policy for Robot Manipulation

Built an end-to-end RGB-D diffusion policy from 245 demonstrations and deployed it on a physical UR5 for table-cleaning manipulation, achieving 66% task success.

Project page

Depth Video Diffusion for Robot Policy Learning

Developed a conditional 3D U-Net that generates temporally consistent depth videos from language instructions and RGB-D observations, with substantially faster inference than RGB-video diffusion.
